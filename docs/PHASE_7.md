# Phase 7 — Town life, quests, and progression

Greyhaven now has an original island adventure with one main quest, three side quests, resident routines, and use-based character progression. Start a new island campaign through Run-Greyhaven.cmd. The existing tile-authoritative movement, interpolated sprites, and ground/backpack/paperdoll dragging remain the foundation.

## Playing

- **Q**: movable quest journal. Talk to a giver within two tiles, then explicitly accept. Track an objective, mark required supplies, abandon, or return to the named recipient to collect rewards.
- **K**: movable skills and character panel. Cycle each skill or statistic between gain, decrease and locked. Talk to Orrin, Sera or Elin to choose a teacher, then train nearby.
- **Y**: apply one carried clean bandage. Healing takes 4–8 seconds based on Dexterity; movement beyond one tile interrupts it and incoming hits reduce success.
- **F10**: meditate outside war mode, with weapon and shield in the backpack. Movement, casting and incoming damage break concentration. Failed attempts have a ten-second retry delay.
- Right-click carried quest supplies for their quest-item mark. Marked supplies cannot be sold, thrown, or discarded; split stacks retain their identity. Unmark supplies or abandon their quest to release them.
- A fallen spirit can walk to Elin and interact for resurrection with 10 health. Equipment stays at the fallen pack. The existing camp recovery option also remains available.

### The island stories

| Story | Start / reward recipient | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Light Below | Sera | Consult Orrin, discover the Broken Beacon, explore both cellar floors, find the key, open the gate, defeat the Lantern Keeper, carry its unique Lantern Heart back and install it at the beacon stairs. Return to Sera. |
| The Morrowwood Road | Lena | Clear the two named road hounds and return. |
| A Spark for the Anchor Forge | Orrin | Explicitly mark three carried tide crystals, then hand them in. |
| Letters for the Harbour | Mara / Cora | Carry Mara's physical letters to Cora, speak to her, and claim the reward there. |

Quests are finite and reward once. Encounters defeated before acceptance still count, avoiding an impossible quest after clearing a finite island. Quest rewards are physical inventory objects. A full backpack places rewards at your feet, following ServUO's fallback. At the global item-entity limit, the whole claim is rejected so supplies and reward eligibility remain intact.

The Lantern Heart appears once beside the fallen Keeper, even if the main quest was accepted later. Do not leave your quest items behind in a fallen pack: reclaim them before turning in. Restoring the beacon gives the original story title Lantern Warden.

## Town and character rules

Fifteen named residents have saved positions. Seven residents walk between work, evening social destinations, and homes using collision-checked tiles and ordinary unlocked doors. They pause near the player and during conversations. Core merchants, quest givers and healers remain at their authored service locations. This is a limited daily routine system, not a full needs simulation.

The clock begins at 08:00. Like ServUO's clock, one world minute takes five real seconds; a day takes two hours of unpaused campaign time. Night has a subtle blue wash. Nessa restocks every sixty real minutes of campaign time. Pause freezes the simulation.

Eleven implemented skills use tenths, a 100.0 individual cap, a 700.0 total cap, and gain/decrease/locked controls. Skill use can improve skills; practice at the total cap requires a decrease-locked donor. Strength, Dexterity and Intelligence can grow through practice, with a 225 total and 125 individual cap. Strength affects weapon damage, Dexterity bandage delay, and Intelligence combines with Evaluating Intelligence for spell damage. Training costs one silver mark per tenth and stops at one-third of the teacher's skill, up to 42.0. Skill checks affect weapon accuracy, throwing, magery, parrying, healing and resistance.

These are selected ServUO rules adapted to Greyhaven's existing combat. Greyhaven is not claiming complete UO rules compatibility: its spells, monster statistics, equipment, fixed resource pools, original reputation title, and island quest text are bespoke. See PHASE_7_SERVUO.md for exact source references and differences.

## Persistence and tools

Quest states, item marks, RNG state, skills and locks, reputation, routines, clock-related timers, bandaging, meditation and beacon state save with the campaign. Phase 6 saves are accepted through the explicit content compatibility fingerprint; town state initializes once on upgrade. The saved map remains authoritative. Old small test maps without the island cast do not acquire island progression.

The catalog is content/original/adventure.json, generated by tools/content/build_adventure.py. It is hash-checked with the package. Editing it requires rebuilding the content hash manifest. Maps retain the Phase 6 schema and editor workflow; this phase does not add a graphical quest-authoring editor.

No new external art is required. Bandages reuse the existing original scroll icon, and the Heart reuses the crystal icon with a unique name and quest mark. Dedicated new art and audio remain Phase 8 work.
