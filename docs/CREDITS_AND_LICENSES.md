# Credits, source and license boundaries

**Greyhaven original project code:** © 2026 Michael Robinson. The root MIT license explicitly applies to original foundation code, not the entire mixed-dependency tree or all game assets.

**ServUO:** source retained in `vendor/ServUO`; the compiled Greyhaven.ServUO.Core assembly and specific server adaptations are GPL-covered. The corresponding source is included in this repository at the release tag, alongside `src/Greyhaven.ServUO.Core` and `src/Greyhaven.Server`. Redistributors should review applicable GPL obligations and ensure source remains available with distributed binaries.

**TazUO:** preserved upstream fork and relevant code under `vendor/TazUO`, with BSD notice.

**CentrED:** preserved source under `vendor/CentrED`, with MIT notice.

**FNA / FAudio / FNA3D / SDL / .NET / fonts and other dependencies:** preserve their bundled licenses and credits. Runtime notices are in the **Windows ZIP** at `Runtime/Notices/`, and additional upstream license files are included in the source ZIP.

**Original game art, maps, soundtrack, writing, and content:** separate terms have not yet been selected. They are provided here to play or inspect the prototype; **do not assume permission to extract, reuse, license, or redistribute individual game assets**. A content licensing decision and legal review remain needed before wider distribution.

For detailed provenance and warnings see `LICENSE-BOUNDARIES.md`, `art/source/provenance.json`, `art/source/phase8/audio-production.json`, and `manifests/resources-review.json`.
